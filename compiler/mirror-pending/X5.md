# X5: what the stage-2 expander, checker and emitter must reproduce (mirror pending, L19 and L20)

Nothing under `compiler/` is edited. The sources of `lib/` and `compiler/` do not depend on the new
behaviour (no `Option` test, no float-literal adoption); they run unchanged under the Rust tools.

| Rust | What it does now | Shown by |
|---|---|---|

| `types/lower/call.rs` `Special::Not`, `special_call` | A call of the builtin `not` with one operand is `(if x false true)`; `not` as a value stays the `bool` builtin. | case 677 |

Nothing else sees the new kinds. | `fibref types`, `fibref own`, `fibc emit-dump` on cases 722, 1505, 1506 |

## Not done

* `when-let` over a `bool` binding (case 1501, `open: L20`): `when-let` still expands to a `match` on `(some p)`, so
  the binding is an Option. It needs the checker to read the binding's type, a form of its own.
* L26 (the integer widths) is not in this package; `(+ x 1)` with `x: i32` is still `cannot unify i64 with i32`.
* `cond->` and `some->` (named by L20) are not touched; neither is `if-not` with an Option test beyond what `not` gives.
* A payload that is a type variable is a plain `some` test even when it is instantiated at `bool`.
