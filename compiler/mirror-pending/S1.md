# S1: what the stage-2 checker must reproduce (mirror pending)

Mirror duty is suspended (owner, 2026-10-02): nothing under `compiler/` is edited. S1 changes no
expander and no prelude; it changes one thing the stage-2 type checker (`compiler/types/infer/`)
must produce the same way, because `compare.sh` compares its `inst` table lines with `fibref
types --tables`.

## Checker (not an expander change)

| Rust | What it does now | Shown by |
|---|---|---|
| `crates/fibref/src/types/infer/general.rs`, `Cx::finalize_uses` and the new `Cx::scheme_instantiation`, `GenMap::n_vars`, `GenMap::gen_of` | An occurrence of a fully annotated member of the unit's SCC (a `PolyCall` in `u.poly_calls`, typed at the member's annotation, §3.6) used to be recorded with the annotation's types only: one entry per variable of the member's own annotation, in the annotation's order. The members of an SCC share their quantified variables, so the member's scheme has all of the unit's (`ping`/`pong` over `a b` each: four), and the instantiation was narrower than its scheme. Now, after the types are put under the `GenMap`, the instantiation is as wide as the scheme: entry `j` of the annotation goes to the `Gen` index the map gives the rigid variable `u.poly_vars[callee][j]` (`gen_of(Key::Rigid(r))`), and every other entry is `Gen(k)`, the caller's own variable by index (the convention of an SCC occurrence with no instantiation). The colours of the instantiation are untouched. `check_polymorphic_recursion` still reads the annotation-width form: it runs before `finalize`. | `types::tests::polyrec::an_occurrence_of_a_member_is_instantiated_at_every_variable_of_its_scheme` (the `inst` rows of `ping`, `pong`: `Gen0 Gen1 Gen1 Gen0` and `Gen3 Gen2 Gen2 Gen3`); `fibc` unit test `compile::tests::mutually_recursive_members_generic_in_a_bounded_variable_compile_once_per_type`; ownership case 245 under `fibref cases` and `fibc cases` |

Effect on the dump (`fibref types --tables`): the `inst` line at such an occurrence now lists one
type per variable of the callee's scheme (`inst E.. e |` became `inst E.. e e |` for two
mutually recursive `defun`s over one bounded variable each). A self-recursive `defun` is
unchanged when the annotation's order is the scheme's. The stage-2 checker's table of instantiations
must widen the same way, in `types.infer.general` where the unit is finalised, or a program with
mutually recursive annotated generic functions will differ in `compare.sh`; once it does, the
workaround in `compiler/types/annot.fib` (`anns` takes its conversion as a `:borrow` function
parameter) is no longer needed by the fault S1 fixed (the reduction, `ping`/`pong` over a bounded
variable, now compiles and runs under `fibc`; the real `ann-to-ty`/`anns` pair was not tried).
