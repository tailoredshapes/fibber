## Expander (`compiler/expand/**`)

| Rust | What it does now | Shown by |
|---|---|---|
| `expand/overload.rs` (new module) `Overload`, `Table`, `scan`, `clauses_of`, `clause_arity`, `clause_name`, `pick` | A module's table of clauses (`name` to the arities of its `defn` clauses and of its protocol methods) is read off the unexpanded forms (top-level `do` looked through) before the first form is expanded. `pick` rewrites a call head `name`, `alias/name`, `fib.core/name` to `name$N` when `N` is a clause count, leaves it alone when `N` is a protocol method's count, else `NoClause`. `(gensym)` becomes `(gensym "G__")` unless the module defines `gensym`. | cases 1750 to 1760, 725; unit tests `expand::overload::tests` |

`Cx::is_library`): the arity error of every function is `NAME takes N argument(s), got M`. In
`compiler/types/infer/call.fib`: remove `stand-in-hint` (line 34), the `library` field of `Sig` (line 19) and
`is-library` (line 145) and its uses, and the hint in `apply`'s arity error. Until then, stage 2 still says
`; use get-or` for cases 803 to 806 and 808 and `compare.sh -c` will differ on those.
